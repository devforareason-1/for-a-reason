## Files
- svg/: scalable, font-independent vector logo assets (outlined paths)
- png/: high-resolution raster assets, including transparent variants
- web/: favicon SVG, ICO, and web/app icons

## Usage
- Use the full wordmark for the website header, proposals, and formal communications.
- Use the lowercase fr. monogram for favicons, social avatars, and compact spaces.
- Maintain clear space around logos; at least the height of the lowercase letters.
- Do not recolor the signature orange, distort, rotate, add drop shadows, or put the dark wordmark on a dark background.
- The orange monogram uses an ivory dot; the dark and ivory monograms use an orange dot.
- Use the wordmark on high-contrast backgrounds.
- Use accessible text contrast and do not rely on orange alone to communicate meaning.

## Status and handoff
This is a first production vector system based on the approved direction. Review logo proportions, trademark clearance, and small-size legibility before commercial rollout. The wordmark is constructed from outlined Inter Display Bold glyphs and is not yet a bespoke lettering commission.
Brand name and trademark availability have NOT been verified. Do not use ® unless registration is confirmed.
